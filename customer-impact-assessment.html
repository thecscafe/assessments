What Is A Customer Impact Assessment?

A customer impact assessment scores the people inside your accounts by how much they affect revenue. It helps you decide who deserves your time before a renewal or expansion conversation.

The Five Scoring Factors

  • Revenue influence: how much this person affects budget and spend.
  • Decision-making authority: whether they can sign or block a deal.
  • Relationship strength: how well you know them and how much they trust you.
  • Advocacy level: how actively they promote your product internally.
  • Network reach: how many other stakeholders they can bring in.

How To Use The Results

Contacts with high influence and a weak relationship are your biggest risk, so plan time with them first. High advocates with strong reach are your best path to expansion and references. Export the list as a PDF and bring it to your account planning or renewal review.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I run a customer impact assessment?

Before renewals, before a QBR, and whenever a key contact changes roles. It keeps your stakeholder map current.

How many contacts should I score per account?

Start with everyone involved in buying or using the product at a decision level. For most B2B accounts that is three to eight people.
